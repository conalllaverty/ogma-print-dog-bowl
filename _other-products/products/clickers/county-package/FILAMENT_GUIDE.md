## Notes

- Bambu does not offer exact matches for every official GAA shade in PLA
  Matte. These choices prioritize recognizable county colour pairs.
- Wexford uses Lilac Purple because it reads as purple; Plum is darker but
  noticeably more magenta.
- Galway and Westmeath use Plum as the closest dark maroon option.
- Check current Bambu stock and physical swatches before producing a large
  batch; screen colours and printed colours differ.
- The custom colour shown in Bambu Studio may use the official GAA preview hex.
  Select the named Bambu PLA Matte spool above when assigning the AMS slot.
